# Privacy Policy

What Do You Call...? · Last updated: October 11, 2026

**What Do You Call...?** ("the App") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and does not require an internet connection to play.

## Data collection

The App collects **no data of any kind**. Specifically, it does not:

- Require an account, sign-in, or any personal information
- Collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics
- Access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage
- Contain ads or third-party trackers
- Make any network requests

All game state (score, streak, current round) lives only in the device's memory while the App is open and is discarded when the App is closed. Nothing is saved to disk, and nothing ever leaves your device.

## Permissions

The App's Android package requests the `INTERNET` permission only because it is required by the underlying WebView component to initialize — the App itself never uses it to send or receive data.

## Children's privacy

Because the App collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either. The joke content is written to be family-friendly.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional online features), an updated version will be published at this same address and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
